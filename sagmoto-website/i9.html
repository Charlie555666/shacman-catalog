i9 Series

Zero-Emission Urban Transport

i9 Series

The SAGMOTO i9 is a advanced light-duty truck platform manufactured by Shaanxi Automobile Group Commercial Vehicle Co., Ltd. (Shaanxi Commercial Vehicle), one of China's leading commercial vehicle manufacturers, and exported under the SAGMOTO brand. The i9 Series is SAGMOTO's advanced light-duty truck platform, combining the proven reliability of traditional powertrains with modern design and technology enhancements. Engineered for maximum versatility in urban and suburban applications, the i9 excels in last-mile delivery, light construction, municipal services, and specialized transport roles. Available with fuel-efficient Yuchai engines delivering 130 to 190 horsepower and optimized for low-speed torque delivery in stop-and-go traffic conditions, the i9 achieves class-leading fuel economy without sacrificing payload capacity. The lightweight yet durable chassis design permits impressive payload ratings while keeping the vehicle under critical weight thresholds for urban operation permits. Configuration options span cargo trucks with various body styles, refrigerated vans for cold chain logistics, light dump trucks for construction, and specialized bodies including street sweepers and compact garbage compactors. The modern cabin interior prioritizes driver productivity with ergonomic controls, ample storage, Bluetooth connectivity, and efficient air conditioning. The compact overall dimensions and tight turning circle make the i9 ideal for maneuvering in crowded urban environments and narrow access roads. Combining the manufacturing quality of SAGMOTO's heavy-duty heritage with the agility required for urban operations, the i9 Series provides an outstanding entry point for businesses expanding their commercial vehicle fleet in growth markets across Asia, Africa, and Latin America.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the I9 have an electric version?

Yes! The I9 Series includes both diesel and pure electric variants. The electric I9 features an integrated motor controller, lithium battery pack, and DC fast charging. With zero tailpipe emissions and low noise, it's ideal for urban logistics in cities with emission restrictions.

What is the range of the electric I9?

The electric I9 delivers a range of 200-280 km on a single charge (depending on load and driving conditions), with DC fast charging capability that reaches 80% charge in approximately 1 hour. It's designed for urban delivery routes with return-to-base charging.

What are the advanced features of the I9 cab?

The I9 cab features a 10-inch multimedia touchscreen, reverse camera, multifunction steering wheel with cruise control, automatic climate control, electric heating mirrors, USB fast charging ports, and optional leather seats. The modern interior rivals passenger vehicle comfort levels.

What is the payload capacity of the I9 electric?

The I9 electric supports GVWR up to 8T with a maximum payload of approximately 4 tons. It's optimized for urban distribution where payload requirements are moderate but frequent stop-start driving benefits from electric efficiency.

How does the I9 compare to the X9?

The I9 represents SAGMOTO's newer-generation platform with updated cab design, modern connectivity features, and an electric powertrain option. The X9 is the established workhorse with more configuration variety and long-proven reliability, while the I9 adds technology and green credentials.
