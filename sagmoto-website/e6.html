E6 Series

Versatile Medium-Duty Commercial Vehicle

E6 Series

The SAGMOTO E6 is a versatile medium-to-heavy-duty truck series manufactured by Shaanxi Automobile Group Commercial Vehicle Co., Ltd. (Shaanxi Commercial Vehicle), one of China's leading commercial vehicle manufacturers, and exported under the SAGMOTO brand. The E6 Series represents SAGMOTO's versatile medium-to-heavy-duty truck range, engineered to bridge the gap between urban distribution and regional haulage. Designed on a modular platform, the E6 offers extensive configuration flexibility — from 4×2 cargo trucks for city logistics to 6×4 dump trucks for construction and 8×4 concrete mixers for infrastructure projects. Powered by Cummins ISD and Yuchai YC6J engine options from 180 to 260 horsepower, the E6 delivers excellent fuel economy without compromising performance. The cabin features a modern ergonomic design with panoramic visibility, adjustable air-suspended driver seat, multi-function steering wheel, and an intuitive digital instrument cluster. The E6's lightweight yet rigid frame design maximizes payload capacity while maintaining structural integrity under heavy loads. Key export markets include Southeast Asia, Africa, and Latin America, where the E6 has earned a reputation for reliability, easy maintenance, and competitive pricing. Factory-backed warranty and a growing network of authorized service centers provide peace of mind for international fleet buyers.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the E6 differ from the E3?

The E6 is positioned as a medium/heavy-duty platform while the E3 is a full heavy-duty platform. The E6 features a slightly smaller cab, lighter chassis, and more fuel-efficient engine options (180-260 HP), making it cost-effective for regional transport where full heavy-duty capability isn't required.

What is the fuel economy of the E6?

The E6 achieves 25-35 L/100km depending on load and terrain, with Cummins ISD engines providing excellent fuel efficiency through advanced combustion technology and optimized gear ratios. The 8-speed transmission ensures the engine operates in its most efficient RPM range.

What body types are available for the E6?

The E6 platform supports lorry/cargo bodies (flatbed, dropside, box), dump bodies (10-16 cubic meters), and can be adapted for specialized applications including water tankers and fuel tankers. Custom body mounting is available through certified partners.

Does the E6 come with ABS?

Yes, all E6 configurations come standard with a 4-channel ABS (Anti-lock Braking System) for enhanced safety. The braking system uses dual-circuit pneumatic design with automatic brake gap adjustment for consistent performance and reduced maintenance.

What is the warranty on the E6 Series?

The E6 Series comes with a standard 12-month/100,000 km warranty (whichever comes first), with extended warranty options available. The Cummins engines carry their own manufacturer warranty, and spare parts are stocked at our regional distribution centers.
