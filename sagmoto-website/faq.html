Model Selection
What is the difference between SAGMOTO and Shacman? Are they the same brand?

SAGMOTO is the global export brand of Shaanxi Automobile (SHACMAN), a heavy duty truck manufacturer headquartered in Xi'an, China. The two names refer to the same trucks — SAGMOTO is used for markets outside China to distinguish the export-grade product line.

All SAGMOTO trucks sold by Shaanxi Fenghan Trading Co., Ltd. are built in the same Shaanxi Automobile factories as Shacman domestic models, with configurations calibrated for export markets (Euro II–VI emissions, LHD/RHD, tropical cooling, reinforced frames).

Which SAGMOTO model should I choose for long-haul logistics vs. construction vs. mining?

Use this rule of thumb:

  • Long-haul tractor (40+ t GCW) → X3000 / X3s with Cummins ISME 420 HP or Yuchai YC6MK 400 HP and 12JSD200T transmission.
  • Regional distribution (25–40 t) → X5000 / X5 with Cummins ISD 270 HP.
  • Construction dump truck (6x4, 25–30 t GVW) → X3000 / X3s dump variant with 380–420 HP.
  • Mining / heavy haul (8x4, 40–90 t GCW) → E1st with Cummins Z14 560 HP and 16JSD240T, or Z3 with Cummins M13 520 HP.
  • Urban light distribution → X9 or X7 with Yuchai 140–180 HP.

What is the difference between E1st, E3, E3MAX, and X3s — which is the flagship?

E1st is the flagship heavy-duty tractor/dump platform, with Cummins Z14 560 HP (510 kW) engine, 16JSD240T 16-speed manual transmission, Hande 9.5t / 16t / 16t axle configuration, 8x4 dump or 6x4 tractor layout, GCW up to 90 t. Designed for heavy mining and oversized cargo.

E3 is the medium-duty flagship with Yuchai YC6MK 400 HP and 12JSD200T 12-speed transmission, suited for 25–40 t regional haul.

E3MAX is the enhanced E3 with upgraded cooling, reinforced frame, and additional driveline options.

X3s is the X3000 series flagship with Cummins ISME 420 HP and 12JSD200T, the volume-leader for long-haul logistics.

Z3 sits between X3s and E1st with Cummins M13 520 HP and 12JSD240TA. All four share 70%+ parts commonality, simplifying fleet spare parts inventory.

Logistics & Shipping
Which Incoterms does Fenghan offer — EXW, FOB, CFR, CIF, DAP, DDP?

All major Incoterms 2020 terms are supported:

  • EXW Shanghai / Xi'an — for buyers with their own freight forwarder.
  • FOB Shanghai / Tianjin / Qingdao / Lianyungang — for buyer-arranged ocean freight.
  • CFR / CIF — for named destination ports.
  • DAP / DDP — for door-to-door delivery to your warehouse or job site. For DDP we handle customs clearance, VAT, and last-mile trucking in the destination country.

CIF pricing includes insurance at 110% of cargo value. Typical CIF premiums over FOB: West Africa USD 2,800–3,500/unit, South America USD 3,200–4,200/unit, Southeast Asia USD 1,500–2,200/unit.

Payment Terms
What payment methods are accepted? T/T, L/C, D/P, O/A?

Three standard payment routes:

  1. T/T (wire transfer) — 30% deposit upon order confirmation, 70% balance against B/L copy before shipment. Default route, simplest and fastest.
  2. L/C (Letter of Credit) — irrevocable, confirmed, at-sight L/C issued by a top-tier bank (HSBC, Standard Chartered, Bank of China, etc.). Adds 1.2–1.8% bank fees and 15–25 days processing time, but reduces buyer risk on first orders.
  3. L/C at 30/60/90 days — for established buyers with trading history, with Fenghan approval.

D/P and D/A are accepted case-by-case. Open Account (O/A) terms are reserved for Tier-1 buyers with credit insurance (COFACE, Euler Hermes) backing.

Compliance & Certification
What emission standards are available — Euro II, III, IV, V, VI?

Euro II, Euro III, Euro IV, Euro V, and Euro VI (for EU destinations) are available. For Cummins ISDE / ISME / Z14 engines, emission tier is engine ECU-program dependent — most can be reprogrammed between Euro III / IV / V at the factory for nominal cost.

Cummins EPA Tier 4 Final and China National VI (CN VI) configurations are also available for North American and Chinese domestic customers respectively.

  • Africa: Euro II/III accepted for unrestricted registration.
  • GCC (Saudi, UAE, Oman, Bahrain, Qatar, Kuwait): Euro V increasingly required since 2024.
  • EU: Euro VI mandatory for new vehicle registration.
Does SAGMOTO make both left-hand drive (LHD) and right-hand drive (RHD) trucks?

Yes. SAGMOTO produces both LHD (default for most export markets) and RHD (for UK, Australia, New Zealand, South Africa, India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, Hong Kong).

RHD configuration adds 7–12 days to production lead time and approximately USD 800–1,200 to the unit price. Specify LHD/RHD at the time of order confirmation — retrofitting after production is not practical.

RHD availability varies by model; X3000, X5000, H3000, and F3000 series are confirmed; X3s/E3 new-gen requires special order.

Can you handle homologation and vehicle registration in my country?

We provide all documents required for homologation and registration:

  • Certificate of Origin (Form E for ASEAN, FORM A for GSP, CO for ordinary)
  • Commercial Invoice + Packing List
  • Bill of Lading
  • Engine + Chassis Certificate (CCC for China, or E-Mark for EU)
  • GCC Conformity Certificate for GCC countries (we ship with SASO CoC pre-attested)
  • SONCAP for Nigeria, PVoC for Kenya/Tanzania/Uganda
  • EUR1 / EUR-MED for EU/EFTA destinations
  • INMETRO for Brazil

Local homologation (type approval testing) is typically the buyer's responsibility, but we coordinate with local testing agencies and provide test samples / engineering reports to accelerate the process — typical timeline 30–90 days.

Warranty & After-Sales
What is the SAGMOTO warranty and what does it cover?

Standard SAGMOTO warranty through Fenghan Trading:

  • 24 months or 150,000 km for the complete vehicle (whichever comes first).
  • 36 months or 300,000 km for the engine and transmission (Cummins and Fast Gear warranty terms apply, transferred to buyer).

Coverage includes manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship under normal operating conditions.

Exclusions: normal wear items (brake pads, clutch disc, tires, bulbs, filters), damage from overloading, lack of scheduled maintenance, accident, or use outside intended duty cycle.

Extended warranty packages (36/48 months) are available for an additional 1.5–3.5% of unit price.
