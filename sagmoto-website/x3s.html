X3s Series

Versatile Heavy-Duty Regional Transport

X3s Series

The SAGMOTO X3s is a heavy-duty truck platform manufactured by Shaanxi Automobile Group Commercial Vehicle Co., Ltd. (Shaanxi Commercial Vehicle) and exported under the SAGMOTO brand, one of China's leading commercial vehicle export brands. Engineered for the most demanding long-haul logistics, mining, and construction operations, the X3s is powered by the Cummins ISME engine family delivering up to 420 horsepower with strong low-end torque, and it delivers exceptional grade-climbing capability and highway cruising efficiency across its diverse range of configurations including 6×4 and 8×4 tractor trucks, dump trucks, cargo trucks, concrete mixers, and tanker trucks. The heavy-duty frame employs a double-layer reinforced construction with 300×80×(8+5)mm cross-section main rails, providing the structural backbone for payloads up to 100 tons in mining configurations. The spacious X-Series cabin offers a 940mm-wide sleeper berth with 880mm upper-lower bunk clearance, multi-direction adjustable air-suspended driver seat, automatic climate control, and comprehensive soundproofing for fatigue-free long-distance driving. Advanced drivetrain options include FAST 12-speed manual transmission or optional automated manual transmission (AMT) for enhanced driver comfort. The X3s has become the backbone of commercial fleets in over 50 countries, with a particularly strong presence in Russia, Central Asia, Africa, and the Middle East. Military-grade corrosion protection, a globally distributed spare parts network, and manufacturer-backed warranty programs make the X3s the preferred choice for operators who demand maximum reliability and minimum downtime.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the engine options for the X3s Series?

The X3s Series is powered by Cummins ISME engines, available in 345 HP and 420 HP configurations. These engines deliver high torque (1800-2100 N·m) and meet EURO II, EURO III, and EURO VI emission standards, making them suitable for diverse global markets including Africa, Middle East, and Southeast Asia.

What drive configurations are available for the X3s?

The X3s Series supports 4×2, 6×4, and 8×4 drive configurations. The 4×2 is optimized for regional tractor operations, 6×4 provides balanced performance for heavy dump and cargo transport, and 8×4 is built for extreme-duty applications like long-haul cargo and mixer trucks.

Is the X3s cab safety-certified?

Yes. The X3s cab meets the European ECE R29-03 collision standard with a high-strength steel frame structure. It features a high-roof design with sleeper berth, automatic climate control, and a multi-function steering wheel for driver comfort during long-haul operations.

What types of vehicles are built on the X3s platform?

The X3s platform supports multiple vehicle types: tractor trucks for container and bulk freight, dump trucks for mining and construction, lorry/cargo trucks for logistics, mixer trucks (6×4 and 8×4) for concrete transport, oil tankers for fuel distribution, and truck-mounted cranes for multi-purpose operations.

Can I purchase the X3s as a chassis-only for custom body mounting?

Yes. Shaanxi Fenghan Trading offers the X3s as chassis-cab units suitable for custom body mounting including refrigerated boxes, flatbeds, tankers, cranes, and other specialized equipment. Contact our team for chassis specifications and compatibility guidance.
