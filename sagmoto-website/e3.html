E3 Series

Reliable Cost-Effective Heavy Transport

E3 Series

The SAGMOTO E3 is a heavy-duty truck series manufactured by Shaanxi Automobile Group Commercial Vehicle Co., Ltd. (Shaanxi Commercial Vehicle), one of China's leading commercial vehicle manufacturers, and exported under the SAGMOTO brand. The E3 is SAGMOTO's popular economy heavy-duty truck platform, trusted by fleet operators across Africa, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and CIS countries for its exceptional durability and low total cost of ownership. Available in 6×4 tractor truck, dump truck, cargo truck, concrete mixer, and other specialized configurations, the E3 platform is powered by the proven Yuchai YC6MK engine delivering 340-400 horsepower with robust low-end torque for demanding transport operations. The E3 features a reinforced high-strength steel frame with double-layer construction in high-stress areas, a spacious ergonomic cabin with 850mm-wide sleeper berth, and an optimized drivetrain pairing FAST manual transmissions with heavy-duty axles. With over 50,000 units exported annually, the E3 has proven its reliability in extreme climates ranging from Sahara Desert heat to Siberian cold. Comprehensive after-sales support with readily available spare parts in major export markets ensures maximum vehicle uptime and operator profitability throughout the vehicle lifecycle.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the E3 platform designed for?

The E3 Series is SAGMOTO's versatile heavy-duty platform built for demanding applications: mining dump operations, heavy construction, bulk material transport, and regional tractor operations. With 46+ configurations, it's one of the most flexible heavy-duty options.

What are the engine options for the E3 series?

The E3 offers Cummins ISME engines (345-420 HP) for international markets and Yuchai YC6K/YC6L engines (280-380 HP) for cost-effective regional operations. The Cummins option provides superior torque and reliability for continuous heavy-duty cycles.

What is the maximum dump body capacity on the E3 8×4?

The E3 8×4 dump truck supports a dump body capacity of 25-32 cubic meters, making it one of the largest-capacity dump configurations in the SAGMOTO lineup. It's equipped with a HYVA front-end hydraulic lifting system for reliable tipping operations.

What transmissions are available for the E3?

The E3 comes with 10-speed and 12-speed manual transmissions from Fast Gear (10JSD and 12JSD series). All transmissions feature synchronizers for smooth shifting, and an AMT option is available for select tractor configurations.

Is the E3 suitable for mining operations?

Yes, the E3 Series is specifically engineered for mining and quarry operations. It features a reinforced chassis frame, heavy-duty suspension with multi-leaf springs, mining-grade tires, and a high-capacity cooling system designed for continuous heavy-load operation.
