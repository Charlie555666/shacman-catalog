X9 Series

The SAGMOTO X9 is a next-generation medium-duty truck platform manufactured by Shaanxi Automobile Group Commercial Vehicle Co., Ltd. (Shaanxi Commercial Vehicle), one of China's leading commercial vehicle manufacturers, and exported under the SAGMOTO brand. The X9 Series is SAGMOTO's next-generation medium-duty truck platform, featuring cutting-edge design language, advanced technology integration, and significantly improved fuel efficiency. Built on SAGMOTO's latest modular architecture, the X9 incorporates lessons learned from over five decades of commercial vehicle manufacturing to deliver a truck that is lighter, stronger, and more intelligent than its predecessors. The powertrain lineup spans Yuchai and Cummins options from 160 to 290 horsepower with state-of-the-art common-rail fuel injection and SCR after-treatment systems meeting China VI / Euro VI emission standards. Available in 4×2 configurations for cargo trucks, box vans, refrigerated trucks, and specialized bodies, the X9 excels in urban logistics, cold chain transportation, and municipal services. The all-new cabin features automotive-grade interior design with a 10-inch touchscreen infotainment system, digital instrument cluster, multi-function steering wheel, and enhanced acoustic insulation. Active safety systems including electronic stability control, lane departure warning, and autonomous emergency braking are available on select variants. The X9's aerodynamic body design with integrated deflectors reduces wind resistance by 12% compared to previous-generation models, translating to measurable fuel savings over high-mileage operations. Designed for discerning fleets in emerging markets seeking European sophistication at Chinese pricing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What vehicles does the X9 platform include?

The X9 Series is one of SAGMOTO's most versatile platforms, supporting dump trucks (including 4×4 AWD), tow/recovery trucks, flatbed cargo trucks, street sweepers, and light cargo trucks. With 55+ configurations, it serves municipal, construction, and logistics sectors.

What engine powers the X9 Series?

The X9 offers Yuchai engines from 90 HP (YC4FA90) to 210 HP (YC4E210), plus Cummins ISF 2.8L and 3.8L options for international markets. The 4×4 dump truck variant runs at 160 HP with excellent torque for all-terrain performance.

Is the X9 available in right-hand drive?

Yes. The X9 Series supports right-hand drive (RHD) configurations for markets including Kenya, Tanzania, Zambia, and other RHD countries. RHD models feature a relocated steering system, instrument panel, and pedal assembly per OEM standards.

What is the maximum gradient the X9 4×4 can handle?

The X9 4×4 All-Wheel Drive Dump Truck can handle gradients exceeding 30%, making it capable on tough job sites. It features leaf-spring suspension with main and sub-spring structure for strong bearing capacity on uneven terrain.

What is the fuel economy of the X9 Series?

The X9 achieves economic fuel consumption at cruising speeds of 60-90 km/h depending on configuration. The 4×2 variants with Yuchai engines deliver excellent fuel efficiency for urban distribution, while the 4×4 variants balance power with reasonable consumption.
