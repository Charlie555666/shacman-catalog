SAGMOTO X6 — The Versatile 6×4 Workhorse

The SAGMOTO X6 is the entry point into SAGMOTO's heavy-duty dump truck lineup, offering exceptional value for construction fleets, municipal works, and light-to-medium mining operations. With a gross vehicle weight (GVW) of 25,000 kg and a legal payload of 12–15 tons, the X6 handles the bulk of earthmoving and aggregate transport tasks in emerging markets.

Engine & Powertrain

  • Engine: Weichai WP10.340E22 (340 HP, Euro II) or WP10.380E22 (380 HP, Euro II) — mechanical fuel injection, no DEF/AdBlue required
  • Gearbox: Fast 12JSD200T-B 12-speed manual with QH50 PTO flange for hydraulic tipping
  • Rear Axle: 16-ton hub-reduction, ratio 5.262:1, rated for 28-ton loaded starts on 25% grades
  • Frame: 850 × 300 mm double-layer reinforced, riveted cross-members
  • Tires: 12.00R20 18PR off-road pattern, rock-resistant compound

Best Markets for X6

The X6 Euro II configurations are factory-validated for Angola, Egypt, Myanmar, Philippines, Mongolia, and CIS markets — where low-cost mechanical injection, high sulfur fuel tolerance, and simple field maintenance are paramount. FOB pricing from $34,000 to $40,000 makes the X6 one of the most competitive 6×4 dump trucks in its class.

SAGMOTO X7 — The Mid-Range All-Rounder (6×4 & 8×4)

The SAGMOTO X7 bridges the gap between cost-focused and premium heavy-duty mining trucks. Available in both 6×4 and 8×4 configurations, the X7 adds Euro V emissions compliance, Cummins ISM engine options, and an optional FHB400 hydraulic retarder for mountain descent safety.

Engine & Powertrain

  • 6×4 Engine: Weichai WP12.430E201 (430 HP, Euro II) or ISM11E5 440 (440 HP, Euro V with Jacob brake)
  • 8×4 Engine: WP12.460E50 (460 HP, Euro V) — ultra-heavy mining and overburden removal
  • Gearbox: Fast 12JSDX240TA iron-case 12-speed with QH70 PTO and shift-assist
  • Rear Axle: 16-ton dual-reduction with hub-reduction, ratio 5.92:1 for 30% grade loaded starts
  • Retarder (optional): FHB400 hydraulic retarder — essential for Andean and Himalayan mining routes

Best Markets for X7

The X7 dominates in Peru, Chile, Indonesia, Vietnam, Kazakhstan, and Saudi Arabia — where Euro V compliance, high-altitude performance, and loaded descent safety are regulatory or operational requirements. The 8×4 variant with 31,000 kg GVW handles 35–40 ton payloads in quarry and strip-mining applications. FOB range: $40,000 to $48,000.

SAGMOTO X9 — The Flagship 8×4 Mining Tipper

The SAGMOTO X9 represents the pinnacle of SAGMOTO's dump truck engineering. Designed for ultra-heavy mining, infrastructure mega-projects, and fleet operators who demand maximum uptime with minimum total cost of ownership (TCO), the X9 pairs a 480 HP Euro V powertrain with a reinforced 8×4 chassis rated for the most punishing duty cycles.

Engine & Powertrain

  • Engine: Weichai M13E3 480 (480 HP, Euro III) or M10E5 460 (460 HP, Euro V) — both with forged steel crankshafts and ceramic-coated pistons
  • Gearbox: Fast 16-speed aluminum-case with integral retarder and QHG50C heavy-duty PTO
  • Rear Axle: 16-ton cast-steel housing, straddle-mounted differential, 5.92:1 ratio, 400,000 km design life
  • Frame: 850 × 300 mm triple-layer reinforced with full-length fishplates in high-stress zones
  • Suspension: Multi-leaf spring (front 12-leaf, rear 14+6 helper) with double-acting telescopic dampers

Best Markets for X9

The X9 is the truck of choice for Saudi Arabia, Iraq, Guinea, Dominican Republic, and Dominican mining consortia — where payload maximization, brake safety, and parts availability directly impact fleet profitability. FOB range: $48,000 to $56,000.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the delivery time for a SAGMOTO dump truck order?

Standard X6 configurations: 45–60 days from confirmed LC. X7 Euro V and X9 configurations: 60–90 days. Special paint, body, or emission variants may extend to 120 days. CKD kits are available for in-country assembly with 30-day shipping timelines.

Q: Can SAGMOTO dump trucks run on high-sulfur diesel?

Yes — the X6 and X7 Euro II configurations use mechanical fuel injection with no ECU, no DEF/AdBlue, and no DPF. They run reliably on 500 ppm sulfur diesel. Euro V configurations require low-sulfur fuel (≤50 ppm) and AdBlue.
