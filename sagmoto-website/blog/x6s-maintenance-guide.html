SAGMOTO X6s Maintenance Guide: Complete 8x4 Heavy-Duty Service Schedule

August 27, 2026Maintenance & After-sales14 min read

The SAGMOTO X6s 8x4 heavy-duty dump truck is engineered for the harshest mining, quarry, and large-scale construction duty in 50+ export markets. The X6s is rated for 1.2 million km B10 life under standard duty, with the 8x4 configuration carrying 31-40 tonne GVW depending on local homologation. This guide consolidates the factory-recommended service intervals, severe-duty adaptations for mining duty, common fault diagnosis, and spare parts inventory for fleets operating the X6s in the most demanding applications. The recommendations apply to all X6s variants (WP12.460, WP12.480, and the Cummins ISZ13 option) and to both Hande STR-16 and MAN hub-reduction rear axle configurations.

Daily Inspection (15-20 minutes per truck)

The X6s daily inspection is more comprehensive than the X3s because the higher GVW and the off-road duty create more wear points and more potential failure modes. The inspection should be performed by the driver at the start of every shift, with the engine cold and the truck parked on level ground. Any Red item should trigger immediate workshop escalation and the truck should not be released for duty until the issue is resolved.

  • Engine oil level on dipstick (between MIN and MAX, top up to MAX if low). X6s oil capacity is 32 L (WP12) or 38 L (Cummins ISZ13).
  • Coolant level in expansion tank (50/50 ethylene glycol/distilled water mix).
  • Transmission oil level on side plug (level with lower edge of plug hole). 12JS = 14 L, 12JZ AMT = 16 L.
  • Hub-reduction oil level (if MAN axle, 3 L per hub, check at the level plug on the hub face).
  • Differential oil level (rear axle, 14 L per axle for STR-16, 16 L for STR-16 with differential lock).
  • Power steering fluid level in reservoir (between MIN and MAX).
  • Hydraulic tipper oil level in tank (between MIN and MAX, top up with ISO VG 32 hydraulic oil).
  • Brake fluid level in master cylinder reservoir (above MIN, top up with DOT 4 if low).
  • All 12 tyres (8x4 has 8 drive tyres + 1 spare + 1 spare tyre storage) — pressure and visual condition. Mining spec 14.00R20 tyres should be at 7.5 bar (loaded).
  • Body floor, side walls, and tailgate visual (no cracks, weld failures, deformation). Hardox 450 wear life is typically 2-3x standard steel but should be checked at the front impact zone and the tailgate hinge.
  • Air system pressure build-up (from 0 to 0.8 MPa in under 30 seconds). If build is slow, check air dryer desiccant.
  • Lighting, indicators, and reverse alarm (all operational).
  • Engine compartment visual (no leaks, all belts present and tensioned, hoses secure).

Engine Service: Weichai WP12 and Cummins ISZ13

The X6s uses the Weichai WP12 family (11.596 L, 6-cylinder, 460-480 HP) as the standard engine, with the Cummins ISZ13 (12.9 L, 6-cylinder, 500 HP) as the premium option for buyers preferring global Cummins service. Both engines use Bosch EDC17 electronic fuel injection and Cummins XPI common-rail at 1,800 bar injection pressure. The WP12 is a 6-cylinder in-line with wastegate turbo and integrated intercooler; the ISZ13 is a 6-cylinder in-line with variable geometry turbo and air-to-air aftercooling.

Severe duty for the X6s applies to operations in open-pit mining, deep quarry, large infrastructure construction, or any application with sustained off-road operation above 50 percent of total mileage. The X6s operators in Australia, Chile, South Africa, Indonesia, and Russia typically fall into the severe-duty category and should follow the shorter intervals. Severe duty also applies to operations with sustained +38°C ambient temperature or with sustained altitude above 2,000 m.

Engine oil caveat for mining duty: The X6s WP12 in sustained mining duty generates higher soot loading in the engine oil than typical on-highway operation. Use a high-TBN (Total Base Number) oil with TBN above 12 to neutralise the higher acid loading from extended drain intervals. The cost saving of using a standard TBN 8 oil is typically a 30-40 percent reduction in engine service life under mining duty.

Transmission Service: Fast Gear 12JS and 12JZ

The X6s uses the Fast Gear 12JS160T (12-speed manual, with double-H range and splitter) for the WP12.460 and WP12.480 variants, and the Fast Gear 12JZ160 AMT (12-speed automated manual with integrated retarder) for the premium configuration and for the Cummins ISZ13 option. Both transmissions are designed for high-torque heavy-duty service and use a single-plate Valeo 430 mm hydraulic clutch. The 12JZ AMT adds a ZF-sourced retarder for downhill speed control on mining ramps.

  • Transmission oil change (12JS): Every 60,000 km or 2,000 hours (standard) or every 40,000 km (severe). Drain when warm, refill with 14 L of API GL-4 75W-90 transmission fluid.
  • Transmission oil change (12JZ AMT): Every 80,000 km or 2,500 hours (standard) or every 50,000 km (severe). Drain and refill with 16 L of API GL-4 75W-90. The AMT uses a separate electro-hydraulic actuation system that requires its own filter change at every oil change.
  • Clutch release bearing lubrication: Every 20,000 km (standard) or every 10,000 km (severe). Grease fitting on the slave cylinder pivot.
  • Retarder inspection (12JZ only): Every 60,000 km. Check retarder oil level, oil cooler flow, and electronic control unit function. Replace retarder oil every 120,000 km.
  • Shift linkage inspection and adjustment: Every 30,000 km. Free play at lever ball should be 15-25 mm.
  • Transmission in-frame overhaul: At 500,000-600,000 km (standard) or 350,000-400,000 km (severe). Overhaul includes synchroniser rings, bearings, gaskets, and seals.

Rear Axle Service: Hande STR-16 and MAN Hub-Reduction

The X6s 8x4 uses two rear axles. The standard configuration is two Hande STR-16 double-reduction axles rated for 16 tonnes each (32 tonnes combined). The premium configuration uses two Hande STR-16 with MAN hub-reduction ends (HTL 14 hub-reduction), which adds planetary gear reduction at each wheel hub for higher torque capacity and is preferred for mining duty and for steep sustained grades. Both configurations use a Hande 9-tonne rated forged I-beam front axle.

Hydraulic Tipper System and Body Service

The X6s uses a front-mounted telescopic hydraulic cylinder (Hyva or domestic equivalent) rated for 196-210 bar working pressure, 8-10 tonne lift capacity, and 35-45 second full-stroke cycle time. The hydraulic system is the highest-wear subsystem on the X6s because the heavy payloads and frequent tip cycles place significant stress on the cylinder seals, pump, and PTO.

  • Hydraulic oil change: Every 50,000 km (standard) or every 30,000 km (severe). Drain the tank, replace the suction filter, refill with 100 L of ISO VG 32 hydraulic oil (or ISO VG 46 in hot climates above +40°C).
  • Hydraulic cylinder inspection: Every 15,000 km. Check for rod scoring, seal weeping, and mounting bolt torque. Replace seals at first sign of weep.
  • Hydraulic pump: Every 80,000 km. Test output pressure at relief valve setting (196-210 bar). Replace pump if pressure decay exceeds 10 percent.
  • PTO engagement cycle: Every 20,000 km. Engage and disengage 5 times cold to confirm clean operation.
  • Tipper body floor inspection (Hardox 450): Every 25,000 km. Measure floor plate thickness at the front impact zone (typically 6 mm new, replace when below 3 mm) and at the tailgate hinge zone. Patch weld any cracks immediately.
  • Tipper body side wall inspection: Every 30,000 km. Check for cracks at the top rail (high stress zone from the lift cylinder). Patch weld any cracks.
  • Tailgate hinge and latch inspection: Every 30,000 km. Grease the hinge pivots, check the latch mechanism for wear, replace the latch spring if stretched.

Warranty and Extended Service Programs

The SAGMOTO X6s is covered by a standard 24-month / 200,000 km warranty for the powertrain and chassis, and a 12-month / unlimited km warranty for the cab, body, and hydraulic cylinder. Warranty coverage includes parts and labour for defects in material and workmanship, but excludes wear items (brake shoes, clutch disc, tyres, body wear plates, filters) and damage caused by operator misuse, overload, or by use of non-OEM parts.

Extended warranty options are available at point of sale: 36 months / 350,000 km at USD 2,800 per truck, and 48 months / 500,000 km at USD 4,400 per truck. The extended warranty must be purchased within 90 days of vehicle delivery. SAGMOTO also offers a comprehensive mining service contract covering all preventive maintenance, scheduled overhauls, and emergency repair at a fixed monthly fee of USD 580-720 per truck depending on monthly mileage band, which is widely used by mining operators in Chile, Peru, South Africa, and Indonesia for risk-managed ownership.
