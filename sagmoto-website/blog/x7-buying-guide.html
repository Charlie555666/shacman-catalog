SAGMOTO X7 Buying Guide: How to Import Medium-Duty Trucks from China

August 24, 2026Buying/Import Guide10 min read

The SAGMOTO X7 is a medium-duty truck platform designed for the 12-18 ton GVWR segment, filling the gap between the light-duty E9 (3.5-7 ton GVWR) and the heavy-duty X3s/X5 (25-31 ton GVWR). With Weichai WP7 engines producing 240-280 HP and Fast Gear 8-speed transmissions, the X7 is ideal for regional distribution, municipal services, construction supply, and specialized vehicle applications where maneuverability and payload efficiency matter more than raw hauling capacity. This guide walks international fleet buyers through every step of the X7 procurement and import process.

Step 1: Define Your Requirements and Request a Quotation

The procurement process begins with a formal specification of requirements. Fleet buyers should prepare a requirements document covering the following items:

  • Intended application: Distribution, construction supply, municipal service, or specialized (refrigerated, fuel tanker, etc.)
  • Quantity: Number of units (affects pricing tier and production scheduling)
  • Body type: Box van, curtain sider, flatbed, tipper, or chassis-only (for local body installation)
  • Destination country and port: Determines shipping cost, import duty, and certification requirements
  • Emissions standard required: EURO III (most markets), EURO V (Turkey, Russia EAEU, select Middle East)
  • Steering position: LHD or RHD
  • Special requirements: PTO provision, additional lights, specific tire brand preferences, cabin heater, etc.

Once these requirements are defined, submit them to Shaanxi Fenghan Trading for a formal quotation. The quotation will include FOB pricing, estimated production lead time, shipping cost estimate, and a list of any requirements that may need clarification or adjustment based on factory capability.

Step 3: Payment Terms and Production

The standard payment structure for X7 orders is a 30 percent advance payment (T/T) upon order confirmation and 70 percent balance payment against the bill of lading copy after shipment. For larger orders (20+ units), a letter of credit (L/C at sight) is acceptable and may be preferred by buyers who want the additional security of bank-guaranteed payment.

Production begins after the advance payment is received and the final specification is confirmed. The typical production timeline for the X7 is 4-6 weeks for orders of 1-10 units, 6-8 weeks for 10-30 units, and 8-12 weeks for 30+ units. During production, SAGMOTO provides weekly production status updates including photographs of the vehicles at key manufacturing stages.

Payment Security Recommendation: For first-time buyers, we recommend using an L/C at sight for orders exceeding USD 100,000. The L/C provides security to both parties — the buyer's bank only releases payment when SAGMOTO presents the correct shipping documents (bill of lading, commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin), and SAGMOTO is assured of payment upon document presentation. The L/C cost (approximately 0.25-0.5 percent of order value) is a small price for the security it provides.

Step 4: Shipping and Logistics

The X7 can be shipped in three configurations: fully built units (CBU), semi-knocked down (SKD), or completely knocked down (CKD). The choice depends on the destination country's import duty structure and the buyer's assembly capability.

  • CBU (Complete Built Unit): The truck is shipped fully assembled in a 40-foot flat rack or Ro-Ro vessel. This is the simplest option and is recommended for orders under 20 units. Typical shipping cost: USD 3,500-6,500 per unit depending on destination.
  • SKD (Semi-Knocked Down): The cab and body are removed and shipped separately, with the chassis in a 40-foot container. Requires minor reassembly at destination. Recommended for markets with lower SKD import duties. Typical shipping cost: USD 2,500-4,500 per unit.
  • CKD (Completely Knocked Down): All components are shipped unassembled in containers. Requires full assembly facility at destination. Only viable for large orders (50+ units) and markets with significant CKD duty advantages. Typical shipping cost: USD 1,800-3,000 per unit.

For most medium-duty fleet buyers, CBU shipping is the most practical option. The shipping timeline from Tianjin or Qingdao port to major African ports is 25-35 days, to Middle East ports 18-25 days, to Southeast Asia 12-18 days, and to Latin America 35-45 days.

Step 5: Customs Clearance and Registration

Upon arrival at the destination port, the buyer's customs broker handles import clearance, which involves:

  • Submission of shipping documents (B/L, commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, certification documents) to customs
  • Payment of import duty and VAT (varies by country — typically 5-25 percent for commercial vehicles)
  • Physical inspection by customs (if required)
  • Release of the vehicle from the port

After customs clearance, the vehicle must be registered with the local transport authority. Registration requirements vary by country but typically include: proof of ownership (customs clearance document), vehicle inspection certificate, insurance certificate, and payment of registration fees. Shaanxi Fenghan Trading provides all necessary documentation in English and can arrange certified translations for specific markets.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I inspect the trucks before shipment?

Yes. Buyers are welcome to visit the SAGMOTO factory in Xi'an, China for a pre-shipment inspection. For buyers who cannot travel, we provide a detailed photo and video inspection report covering exterior, interior, engine compartment, chassis, and a functional test drive. For orders of 10+ units, a third-party inspection by SGS, Bureau Veritas, or Cotecna can be arranged at the buyer's expense (approximately USD 300-500 per day).

What warranty is provided with the X7?

The X7 comes with a 12-month unlimited-kilometer warranty on major driveline components (engine, transmission, axles) from the date of delivery. The warranty covers manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship but excludes normal wear items (brake linings, clutch disc, filters, bulbs, tires) and damage from misuse, improper maintenance, or accidents. Extended warranty (24 months) is available for an additional 3 percent of vehicle price.

How do I arrange parts supply after purchase?

Shaanxi Fenghan Trading offers a parts support package with every vehicle order. For orders of 5+ units, a complementary parts kit (value: USD 1,500-2,500 depending on configuration) is included with the shipment, covering the most common maintenance items for the first year of operation. Ongoing parts orders can be placed via WhatsApp or email, with air freight delivery in 7-10 days or sea freight in 4-6 weeks.

Conclusion

Importing SAGMOTO X7 medium-duty trucks from China is a straightforward process when approached methodically. By defining requirements clearly, understanding the certification requirements of the destination market, structuring payments securely, and planning for shipping and customs clearance, fleet buyers can successfully acquire X7 trucks at a total landed cost that is typically 35 to 50 percent below comparable European or Japanese medium-duty trucks.
