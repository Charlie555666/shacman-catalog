X1 vs X1s: One Truck, Two Search Names

Because the model is so often searched as "X1 dump truck" and "X1s dump truck", buyers sometimes worry they are comparing two different machines. They are not. The chassis, cab, engine range and dump bodies are identical. Dealers and brokers in Peru, Nigeria, Saudi Arabia and Indonesia routinely shorten the name to X1 in quotations and inventory listings — which is why the two spellings circulate in parallel. When you receive a quote for an X1 or an X1s, ask for the chassis code (SX3313-series for the 8x4, SX1258-series for the 6x4) and compare like-for-like.
