SAGMOTO X1s Mining Dump Truck: Built for the Toughest Jobs on Earth

August 4, 2026Application7 min read

When a mining operation loses one truck to downtime, it can cost thousands of dollars per hour in lost productivity. That is why mine managers across Africa, Southeast Asia, and Latin America are increasingly turning to a machine engineered from the ground up for unforgiving conditions: the SAGMOTO X1s 6x4 Mining Dump Truck. This is not a modified highway truck — it is a purpose-built off-road mining vehicle designed to move up to 18 cubic meters of material per cycle, cycle after cycle, in environments that destroy ordinary equipment.

Key Takeaway: The SAGMOTO X1s combines a 350HP Weichai WP10 engine, 6x4 hub reduction drive, double-layer reinforced chassis, and wear-resistant 18CBM body to deliver mining-grade durability at a price point significantly below comparable Western and Japanese alternatives.

Power That Never Quits: The Weichai WP10.350E53 Engine

At the heart of the X1s is the Weichai WP10.350E53, a 9.726-liter inline-6 turbocharged diesel engine that delivers 350 horsepower (257 kW) at 1,900 rpm and a peak torque of 1,600 Nm at 1,200-1,600 rpm. This is not just about raw numbers — the torque curve is what matters in mining.

The WP10 reaches 90% of its maximum torque at just 1,100 rpm and holds it flat through 1,700 rpm, giving operators immediate pulling power when climbing out of pits with a full load. For mine planners, this translates to consistent cycle times even on grades exceeding 12%.

Fuel consumption under typical mining duty cycles averages 195-210 g/kWh, which, at full load operation, works out to approximately 35-38 liters per hour. In real mine conditions — factoring in loading, hauling, dumping, and return — operators report average consumption of 42-48 L/100km under load and 28-32 L/100km empty on haul roads. Compared to competitor engines in the same class, this represents a 6-10% fuel efficiency advantage.

The engine is paired with a FAST 10JSD160 10-speed manual transmission featuring synchromesh on gears 2-10, allowing smooth gear changes under load. The gear ratios are specifically calibrated for mining cycles: the crawler gear provides a 14.03:1 reduction for controlled loading under the excavator, while the overdrive 10th gear allows efficient return travel at speeds up to 49 km/h.

6x4 Hub Reduction Drive: Traction Where It Counts

The X1s employs a 6x4 drive configuration with hub reduction axles — a critical feature that separates mining trucks from on-highway vehicles. Standard single-reduction axles multiply torque once at the differential; hub reduction axles add a second gear reduction at each wheel hub, effectively doubling the torque multiplication before it reaches the tires.

The Hande HDZ300 tandem drive axles feature:

  • Main reduction ratio: 5.262:1, with additional hub planetary reduction
  • Total axle ratio: 5.92:1 — optimized for gradeability and load-start capability
  • Load capacity per axle: 16,000 kg (2 x 16-ton tandem)
  • Differential locks: Inter-axle and inter-wheel differential locks, driver-selectable
  • Ground clearance: 380 mm at the axle bowl, allowing passage over rocks and uneven terrain

This setup gives the X1s a gradeability of up to 35% (approximately 19.3 degrees) when fully loaded — meaning it can confidently climb out of deep open-pit mines without requiring push assistance. The hub reduction design also means the axle shafts carry lower torque loads, significantly extending component life in high-stress mining applications.

For operations handling particularly abrasive materials such as iron ore or granite, Hardox 450 wear plate liners are available as a factory option, extending body life to 15,000+ operating hours between major rebuilds.

Double-Layer Reinforced Chassis: The Foundation of Durability

The chassis frame of the X1s is where SAGMOTO engineers made no compromises. The frame rails are double-layer reinforced with dimensions of 300 mm height x 80 mm flange width x 8+5 mm thickness — an inner rail of 300x80x8 mm with an outer reinforcement of 5 mm thick high-tensile steel.

This design provides:

  • Section modulus: Significantly higher than single-layer chassis designs, resisting twisting and bending under uneven loading
  • Fatigue resistance: The laminated construction distributes stress cycles across both layers, doubling the fatigue life compared to single-frame designs
  • Impact absorption: The double-layer design absorbs shock loads from mine roads without permanent deformation
  • GVW rating: The chassis is rated for a Gross Vehicle Weight of up to 55,000 kg, with a design factor of safety exceeding 1.5x at rated load

The chassis cross-members are placed at optimized intervals (600-800 mm) with riveted and bolted connections using grade 10.9 fasteners. This combination of welded main rails and mechanically fastened cross-members provides the ideal balance of rigidity for load carrying and flexibility for articulation over rough terrain.

Mining-Specific Suspension and Running Gear

The suspension system on the X1s is a reinforced multi-leaf spring design engineered specifically for mining duty cycles:

  • Front suspension: Semi-elliptic leaf springs, 10 leaves per side, 90 mm total width, with double-acting hydraulic shock absorbers
  • Rear suspension: Inverted semi-elliptic balanced suspension with 12 leaves per side, 100 mm total width, equipped with auxiliary rubber bump stops for overload protection
  • Spring steel grade: 60Si2Mn high-silicon manganese spring steel, heat-treated to 42-47 HRC

Tires are a critical and often overlooked component. The X1s runs on 12.00R20 mining-specification radial tires with a load index of 154/150K (3,750 kg single / 3,350 kg dual) and an aggressive off-road tread pattern. The tires feature reinforced sidewalls, cut-resistant tread compounds, and heat-dissipating shoulder designs. On the rear tandem, ten tires (four per side plus two spares typically carried) provide a substantial contact patch that reduces ground pressure on soft mine surfaces.

Operator Safety and Cab Environment

Mining cabs must protect operators from rollovers, falling objects, dust, and extreme temperatures. The X1s cab meets ECE R29-03 crash safety standards — the most stringent commercial vehicle cabin strength regulation — through:

  • All-steel welded cab construction with integrated roll cage
  • Reinforced A-pillars and roof structure tested to withstand 100% of the vehicle's weight in a static roof load test
  • Front pendulum impact absorption designed into the bumper and chassis front section
  • Rear cab wall reinforced to prevent load intrusion in the event of cargo shift

For dust protection — critical in dry mining environments — the cab features a positive-pressure ventilation system with two-stage filtration:

  1. Primary cyclonic pre-cleaner (removes 85% of particles before reaching the filter)
  2. High-efficiency HEPA cabin filter (rated MERV 16, capturing 99.97% of particles at 0.3 microns)

The air conditioning system is rated for ambient temperatures up to 55°C with a cooling capacity of 4,500 kcal/h, maintaining cabin temperatures below 26°C even in equatorial midday sun. The HVAC system also provides heating rated for operation down to -30°C for high-altitude mining operations.

Maintenance in Harsh Mining Environments

Mining presents unique maintenance challenges: abrasive dust, continuous heavy loading, and limited workshop access. The X1s is designed with serviceability as a priority:

  • Tilt cab: The entire cab tilts forward hydraulically (45 degrees) in under 30 seconds, providing unimpeded access to the engine, transmission, and cooling system
  • Centralized greasing: All grease points (24 locations) are accessible from ground level, with the option of an automatic centralized lubrication system
  • Filter placement: Engine oil, fuel, and air filters are mounted on the left side of the engine for single-side service access
  • Diagnostic port: Standard OBD-II diagnostic connector for rapid fault identification
  • On-board weighing: Optional load cell system provides real-time payload data to prevent overloading
