SAGMOTO X6s Buying Guide: How to Import Medium-Duty Trucks from China

August 19, 2026Buying / Import Guide10 min read

The SAGMOTO X6s is the premium medium-duty platform that bridges the gap between the light-duty X9/X7 range and the heavy-duty X3s/X5 tractors. Powered by the Cummins ISD engine family and featuring the M3000 cab with sedan-like interior comfort, the X6s delivers a compelling combination of payload capacity, fuel efficiency, and driver comfort that makes it ideal for regional logistics, construction supply, and municipal fleet operators worldwide.

This buying guide walks international fleet buyers through every step of sourcing and importing the X6s — from specification selection and pricing negotiation to shipping, customs clearance, and spare parts provisioning.

The X6s features several engineering highlights that distinguish it from the standard X6: maintenance-free high-efficiency drive axles that reduce lubrication service intervals by 50%, special low-rolling-resistance tires that reduce fuel consumption by approximately 5%, and an optimized new transmission system for improved gear shift smoothness. Step 1: Configuration Selection

The first decision is matching the X6s configuration to your operational requirements:

  • 4x2 Cargo Truck: Best for regional distribution and urban delivery at GVW up to 18T. The shortest wheelbase and 6-speed transmission optimize for stop-and-go cycles.
  • 6x4 Dropside / Flatbed: Best for construction supply and material transport at GVW up to 25T. The 8-speed transmission provides the gear steps needed for loaded hill starts at quarry and site exits.
  • 4x4 AWD Cargo: Best for off-road construction sites, agricultural applications, and unpaved road conditions where 4x2 traction is insufficient.

Optional equipment to consider during specification:

  • Air conditioning: USD 600-900 (essential for tropical and Middle East markets)
  • Aluminum wheels: USD 800 (reduces unsprung weight, improves tire life by ~10%)
  • Parking cooler: USD 500 (essential for sleeper operations in hot climates)
  • Central lubrication system: USD 400 (reduces chassis grease point maintenance time by 80%)

Step 3: Shipping and Logistics

The X6s can be shipped via two primary methods:

RoRo (Roll-on/Roll-off)

The truck is driven onto the vessel at the port of loading and driven off at the destination port. RoRo is faster (loading/unloading time of 2-4 hours vs 1-2 days for container shipping) and eliminates container handling damage risk. However, RoRo service is available to fewer ports and costs approximately 15-25% more than container shipping. Typical RoRo shipping cost from Shanghai to Lagos: USD 4,500-6,000 per truck. From Shanghai to Jakarta: USD 3,200-4,500.

Container Shipping

For medium-duty trucks like the X6s, a 40-foot flat rack container can accommodate one truck. Container shipping is more widely available and costs approximately USD 3,000-5,000 depending on route. The truck must have mirrors removed and body within container dimensions. Typical transit time: 25-35 days to Africa, 15-20 days to Southeast Asia, 35-45 days to Latin America.

Step 4: Import Documentation

The following documents are required for importing the X6s into most destination countries:

  1. Commercial Invoice: Issued by Shaanxi Fenghan Trading, showing unit price, total value, Incoterms (FOB or CIF), and payment terms.
  2. Packing List: Detailing each truck's configuration, serial numbers, and any spare parts included in the shipment.
  3. Bill of Lading (B/L): Issued by the shipping line, serving as title document for the goods.
  4. Certificate of Origin (CO): Issued by CCPIT (China Council for the Promotion of International Trade), enabling preferential tariff treatment under FTAs.
  5. Emission Certificate: Factory-issued Euro III or Euro V compliance certificate, required for homologation in most destination markets.
  6. CCC Certification: China Compulsory Certification document confirming the vehicle meets Chinese manufacturing standards.
Document Verification Checklist: Before final payment, verify that all 6 documents are consistent — the chassis numbers on the Commercial Invoice, Packing List, B/L, and CCC Certificate must match exactly. Any discrepancy will cause customs delays of 5-15 days and may trigger penalties. Shaanxi Fenghan Trading provides document pre-check service at no additional cost.

Step 5: Customs Clearance and Registration

Upon arrival at the destination port, the following steps are required:

  • Import duty payment: Rates vary by country and FTA status. With ASEAN-China FTA CO, duty to ASEAN countries is 0-5%. Without FTA, typical duty rates are 10-25% for medium-duty trucks.
  • VAT/GST payment: Most countries charge 5-18% VAT on imported commercial vehicles.
  • Customs inspection: Physical inspection of chassis numbers, engine numbers, and configuration against documentation. Duration: 1-3 days depending on port efficiency.
  • Local registration: Vehicle registration with the local transport authority, including emissions compliance verification, safety inspection, and license plate issuance.
