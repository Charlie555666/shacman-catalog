i5 Series Electric Commercial Vehicle

Compact Electric Solution

i5 Electric

The SAGMOTO i5 is a new-energy electric commercial vehicle series manufactured by Shaanxi Automobile Group Commercial Vehicle Co., Ltd. (Shaanxi Commercial Vehicle), one of China's leading commercial vehicle manufacturers, and exported under the SAGMOTO brand. The i5 Series represents SAGMOTO's pioneering entry into the new energy commercial vehicle sector, offering both pure electric and hybrid powertrain options for forward-thinking fleet operators committed to sustainable transportation. Designed as a versatile commercial vehicle platform, the i5 accommodates multiple configurations including cargo trucks, box vans, refrigerated trucks, and municipal service vehicles — all powered by zero-emission or ultra-low-emission drivetrains. The pure electric variant features a high-energy-density lithium iron phosphate (LFP) battery pack providing a range of up to 280 kilometers on a single charge, while the advanced battery thermal management system ensures consistent performance across ambient temperatures from -30°C to 50°C. DC fast charging capability enables 80% charge in under 60 minutes, minimizing operational downtime. The electric motor delivers instant peak torque from standstill, providing responsive acceleration ideal for urban delivery routes with frequent stops. The i5 Series meets China VI / Euro VI emission standards and is designed for markets implementing low-emission zones and green logistics mandates. With significantly lower energy costs per kilometer compared to diesel equivalents and drastically reduced maintenance requirements due to fewer moving parts, the i5 delivers compelling total cost of ownership advantages. Available for export to all SAGMOTO's international markets with comprehensive charging infrastructure consultation and after-sales support included.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the I5 Series designed for?

The I5 Series is SAGMOTO's medium-duty platform optimized for regional transport, municipal services, and utility operations. It balances payload capacity with fuel efficiency, making it cost-effective for government fleets, logistics companies, and service providers.

What engine options does the I5 offer?

The I5 is available with Cummins ISD engines (180-210 HP) and Yuchai YC6J engines (210-260 HP). Both options provide reliable performance with good parts availability and serviceability across multiple regions.

Can the I5 be used as a municipal vehicle?

Yes, the I5 chassis is commonly used for municipal applications including garbage compactors, water tankers, road sweepers, and mobile workshops. The platform's flexibility and competitive pricing make it popular with municipal fleet operators.

What is the difference between I5 and X5?

The I5 and X5 are positioned similarly in the medium-duty segment. The I5 typically features a more modern cab design with updated interior styling, while the X5 is the established workhorse platform. Both share similar powertrain options and chassis architecture.

What is the maximum body length for an I5 cargo truck?

The I5 4×2 cargo truck supports body lengths up to 6.8 meters, while the 6×4 configuration extends to 7.5 meters. The 800×250mm frame provides stable support for various cargo body types including box, dropside, and flatbed configurations.
