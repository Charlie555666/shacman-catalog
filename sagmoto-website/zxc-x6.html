X6 / X6s Dump Truck — Medium-Duty Versatility

The SAGMOTO X6/X6s is a medium-duty dump truck series manufactured by Shaanxi Automobile Group Commercial Vehicle Co., Ltd. (Shaanxi Commercial Vehicle), one of China's leading commercial vehicle manufacturers, and exported under the SAGMOTO brand. The SAGMOTO X6 and X6s are medium-duty dump trucks designed for small to medium-scale mining, urban construction, and infrastructure projects. Available in 4x2 (12T payload) and 6x4 (18T payload) configurations with Cummins ISD, ISF, and D6.7EVIE engines from 220HP to 320HP. A popular choice in Tajikistan, Azerbaijan, and Mexico markets where a balance of capability and cost-effectiveness is required. The X6s variant adds a semi-high roof cab for improved driver comfort.

X6 Dump Truck FAQ

Q: Is the X6 dump truck suitable for Central Asian markets?

Absolutely. The X6 has been specifically exported to Tajikistan (both 4x2 and 6x4 variants) and Azerbaijan. The 220HP Weichai engine with 8-speed manual transmission is proven reliable in Central Asian mining conditions.

Q: X6 vs X6s - what is the difference?

The X6s features a semi-high roof cab (2220mm width) with improved visibility and comfort. The X6 uses a standard flat roof cab. Both share the same engine, transmission, and chassis options.
