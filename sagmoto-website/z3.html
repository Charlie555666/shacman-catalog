Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Z3 Series designed for?

The Z3 Series is SAGMOTO's entry-level light-duty truck platform, designed for urban logistics, agricultural transport, and light construction. It offers a cost-effective solution with proven Yuchai engine technology and simple, reliable mechanics.

What is the GVWR of the Z3?

The Z3 Series supports GVWR from 4.5T to 25T (GCW), making it suitable for small-to-medium cargo operations. Its lightweight design maximizes payload efficiency while keeping operating costs low.

What engine options does the Z3 have?

The Z3 uses Yuchai YC4FA engines (130-160 HP) and YC4D engines (160-180 HP). These engines are known for easy maintenance, widespread parts availability, and compatibility with varied fuel qualities across emerging markets.

Is the Z3 suitable for African markets?

Yes, the Z3 Series is specifically tuned for African and other emerging market conditions. It features reinforced suspension for rough roads, high ground clearance, simple electronics for easy field repair, and widely available spare parts.

What is the price advantage of the Z3 compared to other SAGMOTO platforms?

The Z3 Series is SAGMOTO's most affordable truck platform, offering a lower entry price while maintaining core reliability. It's ideal for first-time truck buyers, small businesses, and fleet operators looking for low total cost of ownership.
